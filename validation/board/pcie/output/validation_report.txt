Blocking issue:
- HLS AXI-Lite control registers are not reachable through the current PCIe mapping.
- /dev/xdma0_control is the XDMA config BAR, not an HLS user BAR.
- dmesg reports: identify_bars: 1 BARs: config 0, user -1, bypass -1.
- Reads/writes to 0x00000000 and 0x00010000 through XDMA H2C/C2H time out.
- Because ap_ctrl and pointer/scalar registers cannot be configured, the HLS IP cannot be started from PCIe in the current bitstream/driver mapping.

Conclusion:
- PCIe data transfer to DDR is working.
- Full board validation against golden cannot complete until the bitstream exposes HLS AXI-Lite control to the host, either through XDMA M_AXI at the documented addresses or through a PCIe user BAR.
