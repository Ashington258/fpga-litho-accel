### 1. Fast pixel-based mask optimization for inverse lithography

- 年份：2006
- 作者：Yuri Granik
- DOI：10.1117/1.2399537
- 来源：https://doi.org/10.1117/1.2399537
- 可支撑位置：第 1 段计算光刻/ILT 背景，第 3 段优化迭代计算负担
- 英文摘要（OpenAlex DOI 精确回查）：The direct problem of optical microlithography is to simulate printing features on the wafer under the given mask, imaging system, and process characteristics. The goal of inverse problems is to find the best mask, imaging system, or process to print the given wafer features. In this study, we proposed the strict formalization and fast solution methods of inverse mask problems. We stated inverse mask problems (or "layout inversion" problems) as nonlinear, constrained minimization problems over a domain of mask pixels. We considered linear, quadratic, and nonlinear formulations of the objective function. The linear problem is solved by an enhanced version of the Nashold projections. The quadratic problem is addressed by eigenvalue decompositions and quadratic programming methods. The general nonlinear formulation is solved by the local variations and gradient descent methods. We showed that the gradient of the objective function can be calculated analytically through convolutions. This is an important practical result because it enables layout inversion on a large scale in order of M log M operations for M pixels.
- 中文摘要（完整转述）：论文将光学微影中的正问题表述为在给定掩模、成像系统和工艺条件下模拟晶圆图形，将反问题表述为寻找最优掩模、成像系统或工艺以打印目标图形。作者把反掩模问题形式化为以掩模像素为变量的非线性约束最小化问题，并讨论线性、二次和一般非线性目标函数。线性情形采用改进的 Nashold 投影方法，二次情形采用特征值分解和二次规划方法，一般非线性情形采用局部变化和梯度下降方法。论文的重要实践结论是目标函数梯度可通过卷积解析计算，使大规模 layout inversion 能以接近 M log M 的复杂度执行，为 ILT/OPC 中反复调用光学仿真和梯度计算提供了代表性依据。

### 2. Mathematical and CAD framework for proximity correction

- 年份：1996
- 作者：Nicolas B. Cobb, Avideh Zakhor, Eugene A. Miloslavsky
- DOI：10.1117/12.240907
- 来源：https://doi.org/10.1117/12.240907
- 可支撑位置：第 1 段 OPC 依赖仿真，第 2 段 Hopkins/SOCS，第 3 段在线仿真瓶颈
- 英文摘要（OpenAlex DOI 精确回查）：Fast lithography simulation and its use in optical proximity correction (OPC) is the topic of this paper. We summarize a model-based OPC system which uses simulation in a feedback loop to generate corrections to the mask. At the heart of our OPC system are tools for fast simulation of the optical and process physics of lithography. For image simulation, we apply a sum of coherent systems approximation to Hopkins partial coherence model and then use lookup tables for high speed sparse image simulation over arbitrary mask geometry. Image intensity simulation at a single point is achieved with O(M<SUB>e</SUB>) computation where M<SUB>e</SUB> is the number of polygon edges in a region surrounding the point. This allows more than 10,000 aerial image points per second and mask image perturbation speeds of 51,000 points per second on an HP700 workstation. A simplified physically based, empirically parameterized resist model is then used to determine edge placements, given the image intensity samples. Together, these systems make up a 'process-tuned' simulation model which can be used for OPC. The accuracy of the overall model is shown by comparing to empirical measurement data. By integrating the fast simulation tools with our OPC system, we can correct a 48 X 27 micrometers <SUP>2</SUP> area in 6 iterations at 96 sec/iteration.
- 中文摘要（完整转述）：论文围绕快速光刻仿真及其在 OPC 中的使用，概述了一个基于模型的 OPC 系统，该系统通过反馈循环调用仿真结果来修正掩模。其核心是快速模拟光学和工艺物理的工具。空中像模拟采用 Hopkins 部分相干模型的 sum of coherent systems 近似，并用查找表实现任意掩模几何上的高速稀疏图像模拟。单点图像强度计算复杂度与局部区域中的多边形边数相关，系统在当时工作站上达到每秒上万空中像点和更高的扰动计算速度。作者还结合简化的物理经验光刻胶模型预测边缘位置，并通过实测数据验证精度。该文献非常适合说明 OPC 中空中像仿真被反复调用，以及 SOCS 早期用于加速模型 OPC。

- 英文摘要（OpenAlex DOI 精确回查）：Many RET technologies, such as rule and model based OPC, the use of sub-resolution assist features, and various PSM methodologies, can be thought of as heuristics employed in an attempt to design improved photomasks. ### 9. Trade-off between inverse lithography mask complexity and lithographic performance

- 年份：2009
- 作者：Byung-Gook Kim 等
- DOI：10.1117/12.824299
- 来源：https://doi.org/10.1117/12.824299
- 可支撑位置：第 1 段 ILT 工程约束，第 5 段边界说明
- 英文摘要（OpenAlex DOI 精确回查）：Improvements in resolution of exposure systems have not kept pace with increasing density of semiconductor products. In order to keep shrinking circuits using equipment with the same basic resolution, lithographers have turned to options such as double-patterning, and have moved beyond model-based OPC in the search for optimal mask patterns. Inverse Lithography Technology (ILT) is becoming one of the strong candidates in 32nm and below single patterning, low-k1 lithography regime. It enables computation of optimum mask patterns to minimize deviations of images from their targets not only at nominal but also over a range of process variations, such as dose, defocus, and mask CD errors. It can also be more difficult to inspect or repair certain types of complex patterns. For these reasons, we desire to determine the simplest mask patterns that meet necessary lithographic manufacturing objectives. Luminescent ILT provides means to constrain complexity of mask solutions, each of which is optimized to meet lithographic objectives within the bounds of the constraints. Results presented here show trade-offs to process window performance with varying degrees of mask complexity. The paper details ILT mask simplification schemes on contact arrays and random logic, comparing process window trade-offs in each case. Ultimately this method enables litho and mask engineers balance lithographic requirements with mask manufacturing complexity and related cost.

### 12. Binary mask optimization for inverse lithography with partially coherent illumination

### 13. PSM design for inverse lithography with partially coherent illumination

- 年份：2008
- 作者：Xu Ma, Gonzalo R. Arce
- DOI：10.1364/OE.16.020126
- 来源：https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.020126
- 可支撑位置：第 2 段 SOCS/SVD 低秩分解
- 英文摘要（OpenAlex DOI 精确回查）：Phase-shifting masks (PSM) are resolution enhancement techniques (RET) used extensively in the semiconductor industry to improve the resolution and pattern fidelity of optical lithography. Recently, a set of gradient-based PSM optimization methods have been developed to solve for the inverse lithography problem under coherent illumination.
